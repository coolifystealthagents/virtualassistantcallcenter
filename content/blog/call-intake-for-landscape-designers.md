## Clarify the design goal

Ask what the caller wants to change, which areas are involved, and whether the request is for a new design, a revision, or ongoing care. Do not assume that design includes construction, planting, maintenance, or permitting.

## Capture property and timing

Record the address or service area, property type, desired timing, access constraints, and callback number. Note the caller’s stated priorities rather than translating them into a design opinion before review.

## Route scope questions

Questions about feasibility, site visits, drawings, contractors, or approvals belong with the assigned designer. Preserve the exact question and explain the next review step. Avoid promising a concept, visit, or schedule without confirmation.

## Keep the inquiry usable

The handoff should identify the goal, location, timing, and open question. Review inquiries for missing service areas or vague outcomes. Update the opening when a repeated gap causes another intake call.
