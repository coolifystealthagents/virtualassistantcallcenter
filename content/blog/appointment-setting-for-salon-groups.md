## Define the requested service

Salon callers may want a new appointment, a change, a cancellation, a consultation, or information about a particular location. Ask for the service before searching for a time. Different services have different durations, staff requirements, and preparation instructions.

## Capture preferences in a useful order

Record name, callback number, preferred location, requested service, stylist preference if relevant, and acceptable days or times. For a group with several locations, confirm the address before finalizing the request. Do not promise availability or a service result before the booking system confirms it.

## Confirm what was requested

Read back the location, service, date, time, and any preparation or arrival note. If staff confirmation is required, explain the next step and create an owned callback. A concise record prevents a guest from arriving at the wrong location or for the wrong service.

## Reduce booking friction

Review double-booking risks, abandoned requests, location confusion, and repeat calls. Adjust the intake sequence where it would make the biggest difference. Friendly language matters, but accuracy is what makes a multi-location schedule trustworthy.
