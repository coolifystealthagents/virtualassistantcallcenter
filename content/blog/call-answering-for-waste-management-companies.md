## Identify the service and location

Capture the account or service address, pickup type, caller role, issue category, and callback details. A missed residential pickup, commercial container request, and prohibited-material question belong in different queues. Specific intake keeps dispatch from repeating basic questions.

## Separate service exceptions from safety issues

The company should define its route for spills, damaged containers, blocked access, and hazardous materials. A Philippines-based call specialist can record what the caller observed and escalate using approved wording. They should not advise someone to handle or move a dangerous material.

## Set realistic pickup expectations

Do not promise a recovery visit, container size, or schedule change until operations confirms it. Explain whether the call is a request, an open service ticket, or a confirmed appointment. That distinction prevents avoidable conflict.

## Close with ownership

Repeat the address, service issue, access detail, and responsible operations owner. If the caller must use a portal or provide a photo through a secure channel, state that next step without collecting unnecessary information by phone.
