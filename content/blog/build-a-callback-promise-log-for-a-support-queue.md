A callback log that records the number, the promise, the due time, and the owner, so a promised call is not lost when the shift ends. The working note stays with the case file for the review period. The lead dates the entry and initials the correction. A second reader checks the sample before it is shared.

## Key takeaways
- Record the promise at the moment it is made.
- Use a due time, not 'later today'.
- Assign one owner per callback.
- Review overdue callbacks at handover.

## Start the callback promise log at the point of work

Document the case, the date, and the person handling it. Enter the source request and the ticketing system reference. An entry absent a source is painful to trace. The ledger should reveal the origin.

Document the the due time and owner before the item moves. Enter the time and the condition found. A later reconstruction is merely a hunch. The ledger should reveal the first check.

## Match the callback promise log to the evidence

Compare the written entry with the ticketing system record and the physical item. Enter every difference and who checked it. A mismatch found after handoff costs more to fix. The ledger should reveal the comparison.

Document the exception and the reason it occurred. Enter the person who approved the exception. An exception absent an approver becomes a precedent. The ledger should reveal the decision.

## Record the handoff and the owner

Document the handoff time, the receiving person, and any open item. Enter the next action and its deadline. A handoff absent a named owner is merely a hunch. The ledger should reveal the transfer.

Document the customer or internal message that went out. Enter the channel and the time. A verbal promise is painful to prove later. The ledger should reveal the message.

## Review the callback promise log each shift

Review the entries by queue lead, reason, and outcome. A cluster may point to a template or a training gap. Enter the pattern and one change. The review should not expose customer data. The VirtualAssistantCallCenter queue archive holds this list for the monthly service review, and a named supervisor signs it before the count is shared.

Check that each entry has the due time and owner, an owner, and a close. An entry missing any of them is the finding. Document the gap and assign it. The next review should show a cleaner callback promise log and less a promised callback that never happens.
