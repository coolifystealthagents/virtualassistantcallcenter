## Ask how the caller wants to communicate

Offer the clinic's supported callback, text, relay, email, or caregiver pathways without assuming one works for everyone. Record the chosen method and any timing preference. Speak plainly and allow time for corrections.

## Identify the device without diagnosing

After the clinic's identity check, capture the device side, model or service reference if available, when the issue began, and the caller's description. The assistant can arrange a repair review but should not diagnose hearing changes, alter settings, or give clinical advice.

## Separate service from health concerns

A charging or fit question is not the same as a caller reporting pain, a sudden change, or another health concern. Use the clinic's approved clinical escalation route and emergency language. Do not decide severity from the call note.

## Confirm the accessible next step

Read back the device, request, appointment or review status, communication method, and next owner. If another person will receive updates, verify that the clinic's authorization rule has been met before sharing details.
