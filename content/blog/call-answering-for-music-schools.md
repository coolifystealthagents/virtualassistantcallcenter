## Separate enrollment from active-student support

Ask whether the caller is comparing programs, requesting a trial, changing a lesson, or reporting an issue with an existing student account. Capture age group, instrument, location, schedule preference, and caller relationship when relevant. This gives the school a meaningful starting point.

## Respect teacher availability

Do not promise a teacher, room, lesson length, or make-up slot unless the school’s schedule confirms it. A Philippines-based call specialist can collect preferences and route them to enrollment or the student coordinator. The school owns placement decisions.

## Make family communication clear

For minors, identify the responsible adult and the approved callback route. Keep notes factual and avoid recording unnecessary personal details. If a safeguarding concern arises, use the school’s established escalation process rather than improvising.

## Close with a next step

Confirm the instrument or program, preferred schedule, student status, and who will respond. A clear handoff helps the school protect instructional time while giving families a dependable answer path.
