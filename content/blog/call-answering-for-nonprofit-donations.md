## Identify the supporter’s need

Donation receipts, giving instructions, event details, volunteer questions, and account concerns should not all share one response. Ask what the supporter needs and preserve the request in plain language. A short classification prevents unnecessary transfers.

## Protect payment and personal details

Use the organization’s approved payment and identity process. Do not record full payment details, passwords, or unrelated personal information in a general call note. Send account-specific questions to an authorized owner.

## Route requests with context

Include the supporter’s name, callback method, reason, relevant campaign or event, and requested next step. If the caller reports a duplicate charge or missing receipt, record the concern without deciding the outcome.

## Review the supporter experience

Look for repeated questions, incomplete receipt requests, and unclear ownership. Improve the information callers receive when the same confusion returns. A respectful closing tells the supporter what will happen next without making an unsupported promise.
