## Pin down the site and equipment

Record the facility, service address, on-site contact, callback number, equipment identifier, and exact alarm or symptom reported. Ask when the condition began and whether the site has an open work order. Keep guesses out of the note.

## Flag reported safety conditions

Use the contractor's approved escalation language for reports involving injury, a suspected refrigerant release, fire, electrical hazards, or evacuation. A call assistant should not reset controls, direct a caller into a plant area, or advise on product safety.

## Include operating context

Technicians may need access rules, loading information, facility hours, and the caller's description of affected storage or production. Record these facts without promising that equipment or inventory can be saved.

## Require technician acceptance

A ticket number shows that the request exists. It does not prove a technician has accepted it. Close with the recorded severity, assigned owner, confirmed status, and next communication step.
