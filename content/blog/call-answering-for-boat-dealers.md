## Identify the vessel and request

Capture the caller’s relationship, vessel or model if known, service location, request category, and desired timing. A buyer, owner needing service, and storage customer should not arrive in the same queue. A Philippines-based call specialist can organize the inquiry while the dealer confirms availability.

## Keep technical advice with specialists

Phone coverage should not diagnose an engine issue, estimate repair scope, promise a part, or interpret warranty terms. Record the symptom or question in the caller’s words and route it to service or parts staff who can review it.

## Make marina access clear

For service visits, ask who can provide access, where the vessel is located, and whether haul-out or scheduling constraints exist. Do not collect access credentials in an ordinary note. The dealer defines the approved process.

## Clarify what is confirmed

Read back the vessel context, request, timing, and next owner. Distinguish an inquiry, callback, estimate request, and confirmed service appointment so the caller is not left with an accidental promise.
