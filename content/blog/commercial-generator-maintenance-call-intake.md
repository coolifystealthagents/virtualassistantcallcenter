## Locate the unit and contract

Record the organization, service address, site contact, callback number, generator identifier, manufacturer or model if known, and maintenance-agreement number. Confirm which building or enclosure is involved when a campus has several units.

## Distinguish routine work from a fault

Label the request as preventive maintenance, inspection follow-up, exercise-test issue, alarm report, fuel request, or outage-related call. Capture indicator text exactly. Do not advise the caller to open panels, reset equipment, handle fuel, or bypass a safety control.

## Apply the documented escalation table

Commercial sites may have life-safety, healthcare, refrigeration, or continuity concerns. Record whether utility power is out and whether the caller reports the generator running, stopped, or unknown. Dispatch priority and safe-site instructions must come from the company's authorized technical process.

## Prepare the technician handoff

Include access hours, gate or escort requirements, parking limits, contact-on-arrival instructions, and known prior visits. Close by stating whether the request is logged, awaiting dispatch review, or scheduled. Never turn a requested arrival time into a promise.
