## Clarify language and service type

Ask for source and target languages, interpretation versus translation, document or live-call context, location, and desired deadline. A Philippines-based call specialist can make the request legible to the agency without claiming that a particular linguist or dialect is available.

## Treat documents as sensitive

Do not ask callers to read confidential content aloud or send files through an unapproved channel. Record the document category, page estimate when volunteered, and secure submission path. The agency decides confidentiality terms and review requirements.

## Separate a quote request from a booking

A caller asking whether a service is possible has not necessarily booked it. Capture the request and explain what the agency must review before confirming scope, turnaround, or interpreter assignment. This keeps expectations precise.

## Route specialist questions

Legal, medical, technical, and live-event requests may require specialized review. Record the subject area and deadline, then route to the agency owner who can evaluate it. Phone intake should preserve context, not make a quality guarantee.
