## Identify the glass concern

Ask whether the caller is reporting a chip, crack, broken window, or another issue. Record the vehicle year, make, model, and damage location when the service team uses those details. Do not tell the caller that a repair or replacement is possible before review.

## Capture location and timing

Record where the vehicle is now, whether it can be moved safely, the preferred service window, and the callback number. A mobile visit and a shop visit may follow different paths, so make the location explicit.

## Route insurance questions separately

If the caller asks about a claim, deductible, or coverage, capture the question and send it to the approved owner. Avoid interpreting a policy. The handoff should state what the caller needs answered, not a guess about eligibility.

## Check the record before closing

Review for missing vehicle details, unclear damage location, and unassigned follow-up. If a field does not affect routing, remove it from the opening. A shorter call with a complete next action is easier for both sides to use.
