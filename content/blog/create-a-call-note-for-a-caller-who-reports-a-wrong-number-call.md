A call note for a wrong-number report that records the number, the message, and the opt-out, so a misdial does not become a repeated call. The entry should be dated and signed by the agent who handled it. Keep the supporting note with the call record so a reviewer can verify it. The team lead should review the entry at the next shift meeting.

## Key takeaways

- Record the request and the source before acting.
- Name the owner, the date, and the next step.
- Keep the evidence with the record.
- Review the pattern each month.

## Record the number and the context

Record the number that was called, the time, and the caller's statement. Note whether the number is on a list or a campaign. A wrong-number note without the number cannot stop the next call. The note should show the source.

Record what the caller was told and whether they asked to be removed. Note the request and the date. A removal request that is not recorded will repeat. The note should show the choice.

## Check the list and the suppression rule

Check whether the number is on a marketing list, a customer list, or a manual dial list. Note the list and the owner. A suppression applied to one list may not cover another. The note should show the scope.

Record the suppression or the correction and the date. Note the person who applied it. A verbal promise is not a suppression. The note should show the action.

## Record the notification and the close

Record the confirmation sent to the caller where the policy allows. Note the channel and the time. A caller who is not told may call again. The note should show the message.

Record the list correction and the review date. Note the owner. A wrong number that is not corrected can affect the next campaign. The note should show the close.

## Review the wrong-number reports each month

Review the reports by list and by campaign. A cluster may point to a data source. Record the count and one change. The review should not expose personal numbers.

Check that every report has a suppression or a correction. A report without either is the finding. Record the gap and the owner. The next review should show a cleaner record.
