## Identify both ends of the trip

Record the approved pickup and delivery locations, department or suite, ready time, deadline supplied by the requester, and verified contacts at both locations. Read back addresses and phone numbers before saving the request.

## Capture the package requirements

Use the courier's approved fields for package count, handling label, container type, temperature range stated by the sender, and any supplied tracking reference. The call handler should not classify a specimen or decide whether packaging is compliant.

## Preserve custody events

Timestamp request receipt, dispatch acceptance, courier assignment, pickup, exception, and delivery as separate events. Never mark a handoff complete because someone intends to do it. Record the person or approved system that confirmed each event.

## Escalate exceptions quickly

Route spills, damaged packaging, temperature concerns, missing labels, rejected deliveries, and identity disputes to the designated operations or clinical owner. Do not improvise disposal, repackaging, or clinical instructions.
