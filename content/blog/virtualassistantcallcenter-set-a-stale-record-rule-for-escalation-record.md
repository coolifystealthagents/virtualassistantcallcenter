## Working context

A source-bounded call center support control note for call center managers: escalation record with clear ownership and evidence.

## Review cadence

A busy call center support team can look productive while the escalation record record is too thin to defend. The control starts with one question: can a second person reconstruct who asked, what source was used, who decided, and what changed?

## Closeout checks

Close the case only when the destination record and the owner confirmation agree.
