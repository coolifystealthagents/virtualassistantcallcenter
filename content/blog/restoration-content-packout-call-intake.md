## Connect the call to the loss

Capture property address, named contact, callback number, loss date, source as reported, claim or project number, adjuster or property-manager contact, and whether mitigation or reconstruction work is already underway.

## Define the request in plain terms

Ask which rooms or contents are involved and whether the caller seeks an initial assessment, packing, transport, cleaning, storage, return, inventory copy, or status update. Keep sentimental, high-value, hazardous, and disputed items visible as caller-reported categories, not settled classifications.

## Respect authorization and safety gates

Record who may authorize access and work, occupancy status, utilities, site restrictions, and hazards reported by the caller. Do not declare a building safe, advise handling contaminated property, estimate salvageability, or promise insurer payment.

## Leave a traceable handoff

Summarize the requested phase, current location of contents, documents or photos offered, access contact, and stated deadline. Route coverage questions to the authorized party and technical scope to the project manager. State whether the request is logged or scheduled, and identify the next owner.
