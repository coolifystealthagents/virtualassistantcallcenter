## Find the immediate concern

Ask what is happening now, where it is happening, and whether water can be safely controlled. A leak, no water, drain issue, and installation question may need different routes. Do not give repair instructions beyond approved safety language.

## Capture the service setting

Record the address, unit or room, access restrictions, callback number, and timing. If the caller is in a multi-unit property, note the unit and the property contact when policy requires it. Keep the details tied to the next decision.

## Use the urgent path consistently

Reports of flooding, suspected gas, electrical danger, or structural risk should follow the company’s approved escalation rule. Record the caller’s wording and the action taken. Do not promise arrival or a technical result.

## Check ownership before ending

Every request should name the next owner and the action still open. Review notes for missing addresses, vague symptoms, and urgent calls placed in routine queues. Correct one recurring intake gap at a time.
