## Start with the caller's purpose

Identify the request around referral versus existing-patient purpose. The opening should be calm and specific, so the person handling the next step understands why the call matters.

## Capture the decision details

Record referral source, body area as stated, location, and availability. Use approved fields and the caller's own words where precision matters. Avoid collecting unrelated personal information.

## Protect the handoff

keep clinical judgment with the licensed team. Give only an approved expectation and record the owner of the next action. A transfer without context creates another call and another chance for confusion.

## Review the next step

Review repeat calls, incomplete records, and wrong transfers for this topic. Adjust the opening question or directory when the same gap repeats.
