## Start with the coverage goal

Ask whether the caller wants a new quote discussion, a renewal review, a policy service request, or help understanding a document. Capture the type of coverage and the date that matters most so the broker can prepare.

## Keep facts separate from advice

Record the caller's stated business or household context and existing carrier only when volunteered or needed for routing. Do not compare policies, interpret exclusions, or promise eligibility before the broker reviews the details.

## Make the handoff useful

Summarize the request, deadline, best callback window, and documents the broker asked the caller to have ready. A complete handoff lets the broker spend the next call on decisions instead of repeating intake.

## A practical close

Before ending the call, read back the key details, the requested next step, and the callback method. Keep the record factual and route exceptions to the business owner who can make the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the first call accomplish?

It should identify the caller's purpose, capture only the details needed for routing, and leave one clear next step with an accountable owner.

### Should the intake call give expert advice?

No. Intake should organize information and explain the next step. Advice, eligibility, diagnosis, terms, or exceptions belong with the qualified business team.
