## Classify the caller's goal

Ask whether the caller wants to register, volunteer, sponsor, donate, ask about accessibility, or reach an event owner. Capture the event name and date so a general question does not land in the wrong queue.

## Keep commitments with the event team

Do not promise a sponsorship benefit, donation receipt, volunteer placement, or accessibility arrangement before the responsible team confirms it. Record the request and any deadline clearly.

## Make the next step welcoming

Repeat the event, requested action, callback information, and preferred contact method. A respectful close helps volunteers and community members feel heard even when an organizer must follow up.

## A practical close

Before ending the call, read back the key details, the requested next step, and the callback method. Keep the record factual and route exceptions to the business owner who can make the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the first call accomplish?

It should identify the caller's purpose, capture only the details needed for routing, and leave one clear next step with an accountable owner.

### Should the intake call give expert advice?

No. Intake should organize information and explain the next step. Advice, eligibility, diagnosis, terms, or exceptions belong with the qualified business team.
