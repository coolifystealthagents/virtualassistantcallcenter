## Start with the event basics

Ask the event type, preferred date or date range, guest count, and whether the caller is planning a new event or managing an existing booking. These details determine the right venue owner.

## Avoid unconfirmed promises

Do not promise availability, room layouts, inclusions, or cancellation outcomes before the venue team verifies them. Capture the caller's priorities and send them to the booking or event manager.

## Finish with a clear action

For a tour request, record preferred times and contact details. For an active event, identify the event date and urgency, then confirm which venue team will take the next action.

## A practical close

Before ending the call, read back the key details, the requested next step, and the callback method. Keep the record factual and route exceptions to the business owner who can make the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the first call accomplish?

It should identify the caller's purpose, capture only the details needed for routing, and leave one clear next step with an accountable owner.

### Should the intake call give expert advice?

No. Intake should organize information and explain the next step. Advice, eligibility, diagnosis, terms, or exceptions belong with the qualified business team.
