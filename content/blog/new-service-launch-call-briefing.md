## Publish one approved launch brief

Give assistants the service name, audience, availability, locations, price language, exclusions, booking path, and launch date. Mark statements that are approved for callers and internal context that must not be presented as a promise. Add a version number and owner.

## Rehearse predictable questions and boundaries

Practice calls about eligibility, comparisons, unavailable features, exceptions, refunds, and early access. The assistant should know what can be answered, what must be captured for follow-up, and what requires immediate escalation. Avoid improvising benefits or timelines.

## Run a controlled launch-day feedback loop

Create a visible queue for unanswered questions and assign a decision owner for brief updates. Announce each approved change to all shifts and retire earlier versions. Track question themes separately from individual caller records.

At the end of the first day, compare the brief with real call reasons, incorrect promises, unresolved items, and transfers. Use that evidence to update the routine before the next shift.
