## Frequently asked questions

### What does this workflow prove?

It proves only that the recorded dropped-call recovery cases followed the declared rule under the tested versions and conditions. It does not establish legal compliance or authorize a broader use.

### Who approves exceptions?

For Map Ownership After a Dropped Customer Call, the client-side service, privacy, security, compliance, or legal owner named in the policy approves exceptions. A virtual assistant documents the facts and routes the decision.
