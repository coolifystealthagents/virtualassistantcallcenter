## Describe the work requested

Ask whether the caller needs pruning, removal, storm cleanup, stump work, or an assessment. The caller may not know the correct service name, so use a plain description and preserve their wording in the note.

## Ask about immediate hazards

Record whether a tree or branch is touching a structure, blocking access, or near a utility line. Follow the approved urgent path for dangerous conditions. Do not provide technical instructions or promise that a crew can safely address the issue.

## Capture property access

Record the address, gate or parking restrictions, preferred callback, and any timing constraint that affects a visit. If the caller is a tenant, note the property contact only when the company’s policy requires it.

## Give the next owner enough context

The handoff should name the work, location, access concern, and urgency. Review calls for vague “fallen tree” notes and missing ownership. Adjust the opening when the same detail is repeatedly requested later.
