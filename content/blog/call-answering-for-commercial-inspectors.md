## Identify the inspection context

Capture the property, inspection type, requested date, caller role, and whether a report already exists. An owner, broker, tenant, and contractor may need different responses. A Philippines-based call specialist can collect this context while leaving scope and findings to the inspector.

## Protect the report boundary

Report questions should be routed to the qualified person who can explain the firm’s process. Do not summarize findings, change a recommendation, or promise that a defect is harmless. Phone coverage should make the handoff easier, not turn intake into technical advice.

## Confirm site access

Ask who will provide entry, which areas are included, and whether the caller has a timing constraint. Record access restrictions and the responsible contact. This prevents an appointment from being “booked” without a workable arrival plan.

## Use a decision-ready callback

End with the requested service, property identifier, preferred window, and next owner. If the firm needs documents before scheduling, say exactly which category of document is missing and how the inspection team will follow up.
