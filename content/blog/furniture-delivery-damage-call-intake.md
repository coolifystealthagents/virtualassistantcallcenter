## Tie the report to the delivered item

Ask for the order number, purchaser name, delivery address, delivery date, affected item, and callback details. If several pieces arrived, identify the item or carton label separately. Note whether the driver is still present without instructing anyone to delay or confront the crew.

## Describe visible damage plainly

Record where the damage appears, what the caller sees, and whether packaging was also damaged. Use the customer's words. Do not label the cause, decide that damage is cosmetic, or claim that a repair will be invisible.

## Follow the evidence channel

Explain the retailer's approved method for sending photographs or documents. Never invite payment-card data or unrelated identity records. If the item creates a safety concern, such as unstable furniture or broken glass, use the store's urgent service path and approved safety wording.

## Avoid remedy promises

Read back the order, item, damage description, evidence status, and preferred contact method. The service team decides among inspection, repair, replacement, return, or another remedy. Tell the caller when that team is expected to review the report.
