## Qualify the conversation without selling a result

Capture the caller’s business type, goal, current channel, service interest, timeline, and preferred contact method. Do not promise rankings, leads, revenue, or a campaign outcome. A Philippines-based call specialist can create a useful discovery handoff while the strategist evaluates fit.

## Separate prospects from active clients

An active client asking about an access issue or deliverable needs a client owner, not a sales queue. Record the account or project name, request category, urgency, and safest callback route. Keep credentials out of a general phone note.

## Protect platform access

Never request passwords or one-time codes by phone. Direct access changes through the agency’s approved secure process and record only that an access issue exists. This keeps convenience from weakening account controls.

## Close with a realistic next step

Repeat the business goal, service question, timeline, and owner who will respond. If the agency needs an audit or discovery conversation before recommending work, explain that sequence without presenting it as a guaranteed proposal.
