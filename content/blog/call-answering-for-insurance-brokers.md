## Identify the service question

Ask whether the caller wants a new quote, policy information, a change, a claim message, or help with an existing conversation. Use the caller’s words and avoid deciding coverage or eligibility during intake.

## Verify before sharing information

Follow the broker’s approved identity process before discussing account details. Record the minimum needed for routing, such as policy reference, callback number, and requested action. Never place full payment information or unnecessary personal data in a general note.

## Route claims and deadlines

Record the incident timing, stated deadline, and exact question when the caller reports a claim or notice. Send it to the approved owner. Do not promise a coverage decision, filing result, or response time that has not been authorized.

## Make follow-up accountable

A complete note names the caller, request, relevant reference, timing, and owner. Review for missing verification and unsupported conclusions. Clear boundaries let the broker answer the real question on the next contact.
