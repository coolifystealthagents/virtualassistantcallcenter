## Separate notice from follow-up

A caller reporting new damage needs a different path from a policyholder asking about an existing claim or a contractor requesting claim contact. Start by identifying the claim status and requested outcome. Do not decide coverage or instruct the caller beyond the insurer’s approved guidance.

## Capture a clean first record

Record name, callback number, policy or claim reference if available, property location, date of the reported event, and the caller’s request. Note immediate deadlines or access concerns. Keep descriptions factual and avoid adding conclusions about cause, responsibility, or settlement.

## Make urgent routing visible

Follow the insurer’s stated path for active hazards or immediate assistance. Routine document and adjuster questions should have a named owner and due time. Repeat the reference number and next step so the caller knows what was recorded.

## Review claim-call quality

Audit whether urgent reports reached the correct path, references were captured accurately, and callers received consistent boundaries. A reliable intake protects the record while helping the person reach the right professional sooner.
