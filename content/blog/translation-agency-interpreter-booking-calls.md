## Capture the assignment precisely

Record the requesting organization, contact, language and requested variant as stated, date, start and expected end time with time zone, location or remote platform, and interpreting mode. Ask whether credentials or accessibility arrangements are required, but do not claim an interpreter meets them before verification.

## Minimize sensitive subject detail

Use the agency's approved subject categories and collect only what scheduling requires. Medical, legal, education, immigration, and business meetings can contain sensitive information. General call notes should not become case summaries.

## Keep matching with an authorized coordinator

Availability, qualification, conflicts, rates, travel, cancellation terms, and final assignment belong to the booking owner. The assistant may acknowledge a request and check approved status fields. They should not identify a proposed interpreter as confirmed prematurely.

## Confirm the state in plain language

Read back the language, modality, date, time zone, location, duration, and whether the request is pending or booked. State how changes must be submitted and who will issue the final confirmation.
