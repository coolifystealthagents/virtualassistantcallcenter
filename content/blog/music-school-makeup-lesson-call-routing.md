## Match the request to the lesson

Ask for the student name, guardian name when applicable, teacher, instrument, usual lesson time, affected date, and callback details. Confirm the school location or online program. Do not discuss another student's schedule while searching for an opening.

## Record notice and reason without judging it

Note when the family gave notice and the broad reason category allowed by school policy. The assistant can explain the written cancellation rule but should not decide whether an exception applies. Flag conflicting information for the program coordinator.

## Offer only approved options

If the booking system shows eligible make-up times and the role permits booking, confirm the teacher, time zone, format, and location before saving. Otherwise, collect preferred windows and send them to the scheduler. Never move another student's lesson to create space.

## Finish with a precise status

Tell the caller whether the lesson is cancelled, a make-up is booked, or the request is awaiting review. Read back the date and time. The note should also show the policy question, owner, and expected response window.
