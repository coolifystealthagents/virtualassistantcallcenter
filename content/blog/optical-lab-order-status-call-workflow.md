## Verify before discussing an order

Use the lab's approved identity checks before revealing prescription, patient, account, or shipping information. A caller knowing an order number may not be enough. If verification fails, record a callback request for an authorized staff member rather than hinting at the order's status.

## Use production stages, not invented dates

Choose only from approved states such as received, awaiting clarification, in production, quality review, shipped, or ready for practice pickup. Copy the last verified timestamp. Never convert “in production” into “ready tomorrow” unless the system provides an approved commitment.

## Separate practices, patients, and carriers

Record who is calling and what relationship they have to the order. Questions about lens specifications, remake authorization, clinical suitability, charges, or a delivery exception belong with the designated lab, practice, billing, or carrier owner.

## Read back the next action

End with the verified stage, its timestamp, any open dependency, the next owner, and how an update will arrive. This gives the next shift a usable record and keeps an uncertain status from becoming a promise.
