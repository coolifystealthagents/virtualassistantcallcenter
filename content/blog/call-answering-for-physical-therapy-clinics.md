## Separate referral and appointment paths

A physical therapy caller may have a physician referral, a prior evaluation, or a question about starting care. Ask which path applies, capture the referring provider when relevant, and route insurance or clinical questions to the clinic team.

## Capture visit logistics

Record the body area or general service requested without interpreting symptoms. Note the preferred location, availability, mobility considerations volunteered by the caller, and whether an existing appointment needs to change.

## Close with one clear next step

Confirm whether the clinic will call back, send an intake link, or reserve an evaluation slot. Give the caller a named expectation and preserve the exact phone number they want the clinic to use.

## A practical close

Before ending the call, read back the key details, the requested next step, and the callback method. Keep the record factual and route exceptions to the business owner who can make the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the first call accomplish?

It should identify the caller's purpose, capture only the details needed for routing, and leave one clear next step with an accountable owner.

### Should the intake call give expert advice?

No. Intake should organize information and explain the next step. Advice, eligibility, diagnosis, terms, or exceptions belong with the qualified business team.
