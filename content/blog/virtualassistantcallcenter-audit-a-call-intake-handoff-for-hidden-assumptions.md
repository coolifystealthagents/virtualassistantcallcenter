## The operational risk around call intake

Most call intake problems in call center support start before the work is done: the request is accepted without a source, a deadline, or a clear boundary around what the operator may change. When the record cannot show what changed, call center managers cannot separate a process problem from a person problem, so coaching becomes guesswork. Write the call intake control around exceptions, not only the happy path, because the exceptions are where misrouted call becomes expensive.
