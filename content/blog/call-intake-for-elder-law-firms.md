## Begin with the caller’s question

Use a neutral opening and let the caller explain whether the matter concerns planning, a family decision, a benefit question, or another topic. Do not label the legal issue for them or suggest an outcome.

## Record only the routing facts

Capture the caller’s name, callback number, relationship to the person involved, location, timing, and the requested next step. Keep sensitive facts to the minimum needed for the firm’s intake policy. Explain when an attorney or intake owner must review the matter.

## Handle urgency without legal advice

A deadline, hearing, hospitalization, or immediate safety concern may change routing. Record the caller’s words and use the firm’s approved escalation path. Do not promise representation, confidentiality beyond the stated policy, or a legal answer during intake.

## Protect the handoff

The assigned owner should see the question, people involved, timing, and open decision. Review records for unnecessary detail or missing callbacks. Clear boundaries help the caller understand what the next contact will cover.
