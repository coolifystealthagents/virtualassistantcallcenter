## Ask only what routes the call

Capture new or returning status, clinic location, broad service need, preferred times, language preference, and callback method. Avoid requesting a full health history before a clinician needs it. A Philippines-based call specialist should make access easier without screening clinical eligibility.

## Keep urgent care decisions local

The clinic should define its route for severe symptoms, immediate danger, and same-day clinical questions. Phone coverage can apply the approved escalation wording and contact the clinical owner, but should not diagnose or decide that a patient can safely wait.

## Handle referrals and records carefully

Record the document category and direct the caller to the clinic’s approved secure channel. Do not promise acceptance, coverage, or a records release before the responsible team verifies identity and requirements.

## Make the next step unmistakable

Read back the service request, location, preferred contact, and responsible owner. Say whether the caller has a confirmed appointment, a callback request, or a document step still pending. That clarity is the real value of good phone intake.
