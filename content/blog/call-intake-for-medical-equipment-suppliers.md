## Sort the request first

Ask whether the caller needs a new item, delivery update, repair, replacement, documentation, or account help. Do not make a clinical recommendation. The request category determines which owner should see the note.

## Capture delivery context

Record the item or reference, delivery location, contact number, timing, and access information when those details affect the next action. Keep patient information to the minimum required by the supplier’s approved process.

## Escalate safety concerns

If the caller reports that equipment is unavailable, damaged, or affecting care, follow the approved urgent route. Capture the immediate issue and who was notified. Do not promise a replacement or provide instructions outside the authorized script.

## Make the record reviewable

A useful note states the request, context, timing, owner, and open question. Review records for excessive clinical detail and missing delivery references. Narrow the intake fields when they do not change routing.
