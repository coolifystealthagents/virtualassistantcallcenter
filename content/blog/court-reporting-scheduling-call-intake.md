## Capture the event, not the case story

Record the requesting organization, caller, callback details, matter reference, requested date and time with time zone, expected duration, location or remote format, and services requested. Avoid collecting testimony, strategy, or unrelated personal details in a general scheduling note.

## Treat availability as provisional

An open-looking calendar slot is not necessarily a confirmed reporter, interpreter, videographer, or remote platform booking. The assistant can submit a request and state its status. An authorized scheduler should approve staffing, rates, rush work, technical requirements, and conflicts.

## Handle changes as new controlled events

For reschedules or cancellations, verify the caller and original booking reference. Record the old and requested new details, timestamp the request, and preserve who must approve it. Do not silently replace the original record.

## Send a complete readback

Repeat the date, time zone, format, location, requested services, and current status. Tell the caller whether they have a request receipt or a confirmed booking. That distinction prevents avoidable day-of surprises.
