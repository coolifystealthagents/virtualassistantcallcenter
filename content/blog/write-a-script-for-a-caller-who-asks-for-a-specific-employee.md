A call script for a request to speak to a named employee, covering the availability check, the handoff, and the message record, so the caller is not bounced around. The entry should be dated and signed by the agent who handled it. Keep the supporting note with the call record so a reviewer can verify it. The team lead should review the entry at the next shift meeting.

## Key takeaways

- Record the request and the source before acting.
- Name the owner, the date, and the next step.
- Keep the evidence with the record.
- Review the pattern each month.

## Record the employee and the reason

Record the caller, the employee, the department, and the reason for the request. Note the account or reference. A transfer without a reason is hard to route. The note should show the purpose.

Check whether the employee is available and whether the request falls within their role. Note the source of the availability. A caller sent to the wrong person will call again. The note should show the check.

## Offer the handoff or the message

Offer a transfer if the employee is available and the caller agrees. Record the time and the extension. A transfer without a warm handoff may drop. The note should show the route.

If the employee is unavailable, offer a message or a callback. Record the window and the owner. A promise without a date is not a handoff. The note should show the commitment.

## Record the outcome and the follow-up

Record the transfer result, the message, or the callback. Note the time and the person who accepted it. A call that ends without a record is a lost request. The note should show the outcome.

Record any sensitive detail by reference and keep it out of the general note. Note the access. A message that exposes personal data creates a new risk. The note should show the boundary.

## Review the requests each week

Review the requests by employee and department. A cluster may point to a routing or a staffing problem. Record the count and one change. The review should not expose caller data.

Check that every request has a reason and an outcome. A request without either is the finding. Record the gap and the owner. The next review should show a cleaner record.
