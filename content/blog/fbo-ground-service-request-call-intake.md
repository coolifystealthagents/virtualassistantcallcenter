## Confirm the flight reference

Record the operator, aircraft registration or approved identifier, arrival or departure date and local time, airport, contact name, and callback number. Repeat letters and numbers using the company's approved phonetic readback.

## List requests without promising capacity

Capture the requested parking, fuel, ground power, hangar, transport, catering, lavatory, or other listed service. The operations team must confirm availability, compatibility, price, and timing.

## Route changes to operations

Late arrivals, passenger changes, special handling, and service additions can affect several teams. Timestamp the change, keep the prior request visible, and identify who accepted the update. Do not give taxi, ramp, weather, or flight-planning instructions.

## Read back only confirmed details

Separate requested services from accepted services in the closing summary. Note pending approvals and the person responsible for the next response, especially when the call occurs outside the station's normal hours.
