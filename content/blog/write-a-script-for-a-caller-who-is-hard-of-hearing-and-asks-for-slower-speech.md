An accessibility script that adjusts the pace, confirms understanding, and records the accommodation, so a caller can complete the task without repeating themselves. The entry should be dated and signed by the agent who handled it. Keep the supporting note with the call record so a reviewer can verify it. The team lead should review the entry at the next shift meeting.

## Key takeaways

- Record the request and the source before acting.
- Name the owner, the date, and the next step.
- Keep the evidence with the record.
- Review the pattern each month.

## Acknowledge the request and adjust

Acknowledge the request without making the caller repeat the reason. Slow the pace, use short sentences, and confirm each step. Record the accommodation and the date. The note should show the adjustment.

Offer a written follow-up or another channel where the policy allows. Record the option and the caller's choice. A phone-only path may exclude the caller. The note should show the alternative.

## Confirm understanding at each step

Ask the caller to confirm the key detail, such as the appointment or the amount. Note the confirmation. A nod or a yes may hide a misunderstanding. The note should show the check.

Avoid jargon and spell names or numbers where needed. Record the method used. A caller who cannot hear the detail cannot act on it. The note should show the clarification.

## Record the accommodation and the outcome

Record the accommodation in the customer profile where the policy allows. Note the access and the date. A future agent should not restart the conversation. The note should show the preference.

Record the task outcome and any follow-up. Note the channel and the owner. A call that ends without the task is incomplete. The note should show the result.

## Review the accessibility requests each quarter

Review the requests by channel and outcome. A cluster may point to a missing option. Record the count and one change. The review should not expose health data.

Check that every request has a recorded accommodation and an outcome. A request without either is the finding. Record the gap and the owner. The next review should show a cleaner record.
