## Identify the request

Ask whether the caller has a question about an assessment, notice, filing deadline, record, or an existing matter. Keep the caller’s description intact. Do not decide whether an appeal is appropriate during the first call.

## Record the facts that affect review

Capture the property location, parcel or account reference when available, notice date, deadline mentioned by the caller, callback number, and desired next step. Collect no more personal information than the firm’s intake process requires.

## Escalate time-sensitive matters

A stated deadline should be visible in the handoff. Route it according to the approved priority rule and avoid promising that a filing or review can be completed. The owner needs the notice context and the exact question.

## Keep the record factual

Separate what the caller reported from what the firm has verified. Review notes for unsupported conclusions, missing deadlines, and unclear ownership. A factual record makes the next conversation safer and more efficient.
