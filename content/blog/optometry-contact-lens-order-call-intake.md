## Verify before discussing an order

Follow the practice's identity-check steps, then collect the patient's name, date of birth if policy requires it, callback number, ordering location, and order reference. If someone calls for another person, record the relationship and apply the office's authorization rule.

## Put the call in the right lane

Distinguish a new order, refill request, shipping update, pickup question, damaged shipment, billing issue, and clinical concern. For a refill, note the product named by the caller and whether the caller believes the prescription is current. Do not confirm validity without the record and authorized staff review.

## Escalate symptoms instead of answering them

Route reports of pain, sudden vision changes, marked redness, injury, or another symptom listed in the office protocol to the clinical path. Do not recommend a lens, solution, wearing schedule, or treatment. Emergency instructions must come from the practice's approved script.

## Read back the request

Confirm the order type, location, callback details, and requested timing. Give only the office's stated response or fulfillment window. The optometrist or authorized team member owns prescription changes and clinical advice.
