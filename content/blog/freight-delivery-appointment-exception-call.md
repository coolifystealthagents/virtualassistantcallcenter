## Anchor the call to one load

Ask for the load, purchase-order, or bill-of-lading reference; facility name; dock appointment; carrier; driver callback number; and trailer number when available. Repeat the appointment time with its time zone. Similar facility names and reused appointment numbers deserve an extra check.

## Describe the exception

Record the current location, estimated arrival supplied by the driver, reason given for the delay, and whether the facility has already been contacted. Label a missed window, detention question, rejected load, and reschedule request separately. Do not calculate charges or accept liability.

## Know which calls leave the routine queue

Use the dispatch escalation path for accidents, cargo damage, temperature excursions, security concerns, or a driver who cannot safely continue. Never ask a driver to use a phone while driving. If the line drops, keep the partial note and mark what still needs confirmation.

## End with ownership

Read back the load reference, facility, scheduled time, stated arrival, and callback number. Name the dispatcher or exception queue that owns the next action. Do not promise a new dock time until the facility or authorized scheduler confirms it.
