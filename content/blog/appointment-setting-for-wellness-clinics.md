## Name the appointment outcome

Wellness callers may be seeking an introductory visit, a recurring service, a follow-up, or a change to an existing appointment. Identify the service and desired result before discussing times. Different practitioners and locations may have different scheduling rules.

## Gather preferences responsibly

Capture name, callback number, requested service, preferred practitioner or location, acceptable time window, and any approved accessibility need. Avoid making health claims, promising results, or deciding suitability. If a practitioner must confirm the request, make that next step explicit.

## Confirm the practical details

Repeat the service, provider, location, date, time, arrival instruction, and outstanding confirmation. For an unconfirmed request, create an owned callback rather than implying a booking. The caller should know exactly what is settled and what remains pending.

## Improve the booking experience

Review no-shows, wrong-location requests, repeat calls, and incomplete preferences. Fix the intake point that caused the issue. A calm, precise scheduling conversation builds confidence before the visitor ever arrives.
