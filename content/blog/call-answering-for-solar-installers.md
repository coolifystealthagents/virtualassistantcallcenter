## Sort the reason for the call

Separate a new consultation, a question about an active project, a monitoring concern, and a service request. Ask for the caller’s goal before collecting technical details. A clear category keeps the handoff from landing with the wrong team.

## Capture property and contact details

Record the service address, best callback number, current project reference when available, and preferred timing. Note whether the caller is an owner, tenant, or property representative when that changes who can approve the next step.

## Keep technical answers bounded

Use approved information for routine questions. Send design, electrical, utility, warranty, and performance questions to the responsible owner. Do not estimate system output or promise an inspection before the team has reviewed the request.

## Improve follow-up ownership

Review whether each note names the reason, property, owner, and next action. Missing project references and vague service descriptions are useful signals for a small change to the opening. Keep optional questions out of the call until they matter.
