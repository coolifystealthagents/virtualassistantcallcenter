## Start with the caller's purpose

Identify the request around caller role and property unit. The opening should be calm and specific, so the person handling the next step understands why the call matters.

## Capture the decision details

Record callback, subject, outcome, and observable maintenance facts. Use approved fields and the caller's own words where precision matters. Avoid collecting unrelated personal information.

## Protect the handoff

route emergencies to named contacts. Give only an approved expectation and record the owner of the next action. A transfer without context creates another call and another chance for confusion.

## Review the next step

Review repeat calls, incomplete records, and wrong transfers for this topic. Adjust the opening question or directory when the same gap repeats.
