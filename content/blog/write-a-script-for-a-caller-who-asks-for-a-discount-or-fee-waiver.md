A fee-request script that acknowledges the request, checks the policy, and records the decision, so a waiver is not promised outside the agent's authority. The entry should be dated and signed by the agent who handled it. Keep the supporting note with the call record so a reviewer can verify it. The team lead should review the entry at the next shift meeting.

## Key takeaways

- Record the request and the source before acting.
- Name the owner, the date, and the next step.
- Keep the evidence with the record.
- Review the pattern each month.

## Record the request and the reason

Record the caller, the account, the fee, and the reason for the request. Note the amount and the date. A waiver without a reason cannot be reviewed. The note should show the context.

Check the policy and the approval level for the fee. Note the source and the person who can approve. An agent who promises a waiver outside the policy creates a dispute. The note should show the limit.

## Offer the available option

Offer the option the policy allows, such as a payment plan, a one-time credit, or a review. Record the option and the condition. A refusal with no alternative feels arbitrary. The note should show the path.

Record the caller's response and any evidence they provide. Note the date. A decision made without the evidence may be reversed. The note should show the basis.

## Record the decision and the follow-up

Record the decision, the approver, and the effective date. Note the customer notification. A decision without a notice is incomplete. The note should show the message.

Record the billing adjustment and the date. Note the owner. A promise that is not in the bill is not kept. The note should show the transaction.

## Review the fee requests each month

Review the requests by fee type and outcome. A cluster may point to a policy or a communication gap. Record the count and one change. The review should not expose customer data.

Check that every request has a decision and an owner. A request without either is the finding. Record the gap and the owner. The next review should show a cleaner record.
