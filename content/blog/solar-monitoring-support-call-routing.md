## Identify the system and the alert

Capture the account or site through the approved verification process, service address, callback number, equipment identifier if visible, and the exact alert text. Ask when the caller first noticed the issue and where it appears.

## Separate monitoring from diagnosis

A blank dashboard, low production reading, offline device, or inverter message can have several causes. The assistant should record the evidence and check approved service status. It should not diagnose wiring, direct electrical work, or tell a caller that equipment is safe.

## Check known administrative causes

Follow the script for account access, app status, open tickets, planned outages, permission to operate, and recent router changes. Keep customer observations distinct from facts shown in the company's system.

## Give the current case status

Tell the caller whether the case is open, assigned, waiting for information, or accepted for a visit. Do not promise recovered energy, savings, warranty coverage, or a repair date that an authorized team has not confirmed.
