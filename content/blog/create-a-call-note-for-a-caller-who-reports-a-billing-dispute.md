A billing dispute note that records the charge, the caller's account, the evidence, and the owner, so the next agent does not ask the same questions. The working note stays with the case file for the review period. The lead dates the entry and initials the correction. A second reader checks the sample before it is shared.

## Key takeaways
- Record the charge date and amount before the story.
- Ask for the document that shows the charge.
- Name the owner who will review it.
- Give the caller a reference and a due time.

## Start the billing dispute note at the point of work

Document the case, the date, and the person handling it. Enter the source request and the billing system reference. An entry absent a source is painful to trace. The ledger should reveal the origin.

Document the the charge and account before the item moves. Enter the time and the condition found. A later reconstruction is merely a hunch. The ledger should reveal the first check.

## Match the billing dispute note to the evidence

Compare the written entry with the billing system record and the physical item. Enter every difference and who checked it. A mismatch found after handoff costs more to fix. The ledger should reveal the comparison.

Document the exception and the reason it occurred. Enter the person who approved the exception. An exception absent an approver becomes a precedent. The ledger should reveal the decision.

## Record the handoff and the owner

Document the handoff time, the receiving person, and any open item. Enter the next action and its deadline. A handoff absent a named owner is merely a hunch. The ledger should reveal the transfer.

Document the customer or internal message that went out. Enter the channel and the time. A verbal promise is painful to prove later. The ledger should reveal the message.

## Review the billing dispute note each call

Review the entries by billing support lead, reason, and outcome. A cluster may point to a template or a training gap. Enter the pattern and one change. The review should not expose customer data. Check that each entry has the charge and account, an owner, and a close. An entry missing any of them is the finding. Document the gap and assign it. The next review should show a cleaner billing dispute note and less a dispute reopened on every call.
