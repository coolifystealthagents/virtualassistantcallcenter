## Confirm the callback can reach the caller

Ask for the caller's name, callback number, location or account reference when relevant, and a brief description of what is happening. Read the number back one digit at a time. If the caller is using a different phone than the account record, note that difference without treating it as proof of identity.

## Apply the approved urgency rule

Match the reported situation to the business's written after-hours criteria. Do not diagnose a technical, medical, legal, or safety problem. When there is immediate danger, use the approved emergency wording and direct the caller to the appropriate public emergency service before continuing the business callback process.

## Close the ownership loop

Route the record to the named on-call owner and state only a response window the business has approved. Record the time sent, channel, owner, and acknowledgment. If the owner does not accept the handoff by the checkpoint, escalate to the backup rather than placing the item in an unowned general queue.

Review missed windows each morning. Separate incorrect urgency classification, unreachable callers, and owner delays so the team can fix the right part of the routine.
