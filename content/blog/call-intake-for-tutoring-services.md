## Understand the learning request

Ask who needs support, which subject is involved, and what the caller hopes to accomplish. A request for homework help differs from exam preparation or ongoing instruction. Use the caller’s own description instead of assigning a level that has not been confirmed.

## Capture schedule and format

Record preferred days, time zone, meeting format, location when relevant, and the best callback number. If a parent or another responsible adult is arranging the session, note who should receive the next message. Do not collect school records or unnecessary personal details.

## Route questions that need a specialist

Availability, instructor fit, accommodations, and safeguarding questions may require a designated owner. Explain that the request needs review and attach the exact question to the handoff. Avoid promising a match before the team checks the appropriate information.

## Make the inquiry easy to continue

The next person should see the subject, learner goal, schedule, format, and open question in one note. Review calls for missing time zones and unclear ownership. Update the intake path when those gaps cause a second call.
