A dropped-transfer note that records the original agent, the destination, the point of failure, and the callback, so the caller does not have to start over. The entry should be dated and signed by the agent who handled it. Keep the supporting note with the call record so a reviewer can verify it. The team lead should review the entry at the next shift meeting.

## Key takeaways

- Record the request and the source before acting.
- Name the owner, the date, and the next step.
- Keep the evidence with the record.
- Review the pattern each month.

## Record the transfer and the drop

Record the caller, the original agent, the destination, and the time of the drop. Note the number and the reason for the transfer. A drop without the route is hard to trace. The note should show the path.

Record what the caller heard, such as silence, a busy signal, or a voicemail. Note the time. A symptom helps the team find the cause. The note should show the evidence.

## Call the caller back or route the case

Record the callback attempt and the result. Note the number and the time. A caller who is not called back will call again. The note should show the attempt.

If the callback fails, record the message and the next step. Note the owner. A dropped call that is not owned is a lost contact. The note should show the route.

## Record the case and the follow-up

Record the case or ticket and the destination that will own it. Note the time. A dropped transfer that is recreated without a record loses the history. The note should show the handoff.

Record the resolution and the caller's response. Note the date and the owner. A call closed without a resolution may return. The note should show the outcome.

## Review the dropped transfers each week

Review the drops by agent, destination, and time. A cluster may point to a queue or a system problem. Record the count and one change. The review should not rank agents.

Check that every drop has a callback or a case. A drop without either is the finding. Record the gap and the owner. The next review should show a cleaner record.
