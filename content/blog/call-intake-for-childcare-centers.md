## Understand the family’s request

Enrollment, attendance, pickup changes, billing questions, and general information need different owners. Ask what the caller needs before asking for details. Keep the wording respectful and avoid discussing a child with an unauthorized person.

## Confirm identity and callback rules

Use the center’s approved verification process. Record the minimum information needed to route the request, including child or classroom context only when policy permits. Never place private family details in a general note without a reason.

## Escalate immediate concerns

Follow the center’s approved path for a safety concern, illness report, missing pickup authorization, or another urgent matter. Record the caller’s words and the person notified. Do not investigate or provide a judgment during intake.

## Leave a clear next action

The receiving owner needs the request, relevant classroom or schedule context, callback details, and timing. Review calls for weak verification or unclear ownership. Small, consistent boundaries make the phone path easier for families to trust.
