## Check for an open request before creating another

Search with the approved identifiers and confirm that the record belongs to the caller before discussing it. Match the issue, not just the phone number. Shared numbers, similar names, and unrelated requests can make an apparent duplicate a separate case.

## Preserve what changed

Link the new contact to the open item and add the contact time, channel, caller's new information, and any change in urgency or requested outcome. Do not overwrite the original wording or reset the age of the first request.

## Keep one accountable next step

Confirm the current owner and response window. If the repeat contact reveals a missed promise or defined escalation condition, route it through that path and tell the caller what changed. Avoid sending parallel messages to several owners unless the escalation rule requires it.

Report repeat-contact rate alongside resolution time. A falling backlog can hide poor service if customers must call several times before an owner responds.
