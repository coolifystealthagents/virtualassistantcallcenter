## Gather the details that change the route

Ask whether the caller is new or established, which provider or location they need, and whether the request concerns an exam, eyewear, records, or an existing order. These distinctions let a Philippines-based call specialist send a useful summary instead of a generic callback.

## Keep clinical judgment with the practice

An answering service can recognize that the practice’s urgent-eye protocol applies, but it should not diagnose or advise treatment. The practice must supply approved wording for immediate symptoms and define when the caller should be directed to emergency care.

## Make eyewear calls actionable

For an order or repair question, capture the order reference, item, location, and preferred callback route. Avoid promising a remake, delivery date, or insurance outcome until the optical team confirms the decision.

## Close the loop

Read back the appointment request or service issue and name the next owner. A reliable call record includes the minimum necessary details, a time-sensitive flag when applicable, and the caller’s preferred contact method.
