## Separate admissions from student support

Ask whether the caller is a prospective family, current parent, student, staff member, or vendor. Capture grade or program, campus, broad request, and callback route. This keeps enrollment questions away from attendance or safeguarding channels.

## Protect student information

Do not disclose attendance, grades, schedules, or records until the school’s identity and authorization process is complete. A Philippines-based call specialist can record the request and explain the school’s secure next step without confirming private details.

## Define urgent escalation

The school must provide a specific route for immediate safety concerns, medical emergencies, and safeguarding reports. Phone coverage should use that approved path and avoid investigating or making promises. The responsible school leader owns the response.

## End with a named owner

Read back the campus, caller relationship, request category, preferred callback method, and next owner. Families should understand whether a visit, callback, or secure document submission is the next action.
