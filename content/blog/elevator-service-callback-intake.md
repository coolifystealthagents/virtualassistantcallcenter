## Ask the safety question first

Determine whether anyone reports being trapped, injured, or in immediate danger. Follow the service provider's approved emergency route at once. A phone assistant must not coach a rescue, tell occupants to force doors, or represent that help is on the way before dispatch acceptance is verified.

## Locate the exact unit

Capture the property name and address, building, elevator or asset number, affected floor, callback contact, and what the caller observed. Note whether the unit is stopped, intermittent, or already taken out of service, without giving operating instructions.

## Preserve each status transition

Requested, escalated, accepted, dispatched, on site, and closed are separate states. Record who supplied the state and when. Do not infer arrival time or restoration from a ticket alone.

## Give the next shift a usable record

Read back the unit, condition reported, site access, current status, and next owner. For repeat callers, add the new observation and timestamp instead of overwriting the earlier report.
