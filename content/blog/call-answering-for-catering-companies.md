## Capture the event before the menu

Ask the event date, venue, guest range, caller role, service style, and desired response. A wedding planner, office coordinator, and private host may need different follow-up. A Philippines-based call specialist can organize the inquiry while the caterer confirms availability and scope.

## Distinguish inquiry from confirmed order

Do not promise a date, menu, dietary accommodation, delivery window, or guest count change until the responsible coordinator confirms it. Record the request and explain what must be reviewed. This avoids turning a hopeful conversation into a false commitment.

## Route food-safety questions carefully

Capture the allergy or dietary concern at a high level and direct it to the caterer’s designated kitchen or event owner. Avoid guaranteeing that a kitchen is free of an allergen or advising a guest about safety.

## End with the next decision

Repeat the event details, requested service, deadline, and callback route. State whether the next step is a consultation, menu review, availability check, or confirmation from the event coordinator.
