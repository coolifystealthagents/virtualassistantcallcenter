## Headline finding

Intent capture is reliable when the note preserves the caller’s request, confidence, confirmation, and next owner. More fields do not automatically make a record more useful.

## Methodology

Define a small intent vocabulary, the minimum necessary fields, confirmation language, ambiguity rules, and escalation owner. Review a stratified sample of ordinary, ambiguous, accessibility-related, and sensitive calls against those definitions. Report exclusions and disagreements instead of treating them as successful captures.

## Key findings

- Measure agreement between the caller’s stated need and the final disposition separately from note completeness.
- Use read-back and correction opportunities before routing an ambiguous request.
- Keep sensitive details out of general notes unless an approved owner requires them.

## Research interpretation

The NIST Privacy Framework supports purpose limitation and data processing awareness, while WCAG provides a useful lens for understandable, operable communication. Neither source establishes a universal call-intake accuracy benchmark. A local review should preserve the source wording, correction event, route, and exception reason.

## Boundaries

A receptionist may clarify a request and route it under written rules. The business owner retains authority for clinical, legal, financial, identity, safety, and refund decisions. Do not diagnose, authenticate beyond approved controls, or promise a result that the owner has not authorized.
