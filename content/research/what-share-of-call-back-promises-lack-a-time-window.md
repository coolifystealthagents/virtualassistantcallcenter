## Key takeaways

- Use the call record, not the caller, as the unit of review.
- Separate a missing field from a field that is present but weak.
- Require a dated source for every counted entry.
- Report the share by shift and by queue.
- Treat the result as a local record check.

This review asks how often the call record lacks the element named in the title. The unit is one call record for one caller. The evidence is the note, the recording metadata, and the disposition. The result describes documentation practice in one team and one period.

## Question and scope

Include calls handled during the review window that reached an agent. Exclude abandoned calls and calls that never produced a record. Record the queue, the shift, and the disposition. If the team changed its note template during the period, split the sample.

## Definitions and evidence standard

Define the element before reviewing any call. A value without a date, an owner, or a source is not complete. Record the missing element separately from an element that is present but unclear. The rule should be fixed before the sample is scored.

## Sampling and measurement

Draw the sample from the call system export, not from the calls the supervisor remembers. Record the export date and the filter. Measure the number of records, the share with the element, and the share with a dated source. Report the counts by queue and by shift.

## Findings and interpretation

Present the distribution before naming a cause. A cluster on one shift may point to a template, while a cluster in one queue may point to a training gap. Read a few records behind the largest group before drawing a conclusion. Record the time as well as the date so the sequence can be reconstructed.

## Limitations

The review measures the record, not the caller's experience or the legality of the call. A complete note can still hide a poor conversation, and a missing field does not prove misconduct. The sample is local and should not be generalized. Report the definitions and counts so another team can repeat the work.

## Practical next step

Choose one repair, such as a required reason field or a weekly note review. Name the owner and the start date. Define the measure that will show whether it worked, using the same categories. Test it on the next set of calls.
