## Research question

This study asks whether promised callback windows match accepted ownership and documented caller outcomes. It treats callback delay as an observable sequence of events, not a single success percentage.

## Evidence table

| Evidence | Review question | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| Source event | Can another reviewer locate it? | Copied note with no authoritative link |
| Ownership | Did an authorized destination accept? | Ringing or answer treated as acceptance |
