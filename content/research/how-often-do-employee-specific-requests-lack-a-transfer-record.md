How often do employee-specific requests lack a transfer record? The entry should be dated and signed by the agent who handled it. Keep the supporting note with the call record so a reviewer can verify it. The team lead should review the entry at the next shift meeting.

## Key takeaways

- Use the call record, not the caller, as the unit.
- Separate a missing field from a weak entry.
- Require a dated source.
- Report the share by shift and by queue.

This review asks how often the call record includes the element named in the title. The unit is one call record for one caller. The evidence is the note, the disposition, and the review record. The result describes one team and one period.

## Question and scope

Include calls handled during the review window that produced a record. Exclude abandoned calls and calls that were never routed. Record the queue, the shift, and the disposition. If the team changes its note template, split the sample.

## Definitions and evidence

Define the element before reviewing any call. A value without a date, an owner, or a source is not complete. Record a missing element separately from an element that is present but unclear. Record the time as well as the date so the sequence can be reconstructed.

## Sampling and measurement

Draw the sample from the call system export, not from the calls the supervisor remembers. Record the export date and the filter. Measure the number of records, the share with the element, and the share with a dated source. Report the counts by queue and by shift.

## Findings and reporting

Present the distribution before naming a cause. A cluster on one shift may point to a template, while a cluster in one queue may point to a training gap. Read a few records behind the largest group. Note the person who resolved the item and the date it closed.

## Limits and next action

The review measures the record, not the caller experience or the legality of the call. A complete note can still hide a poor conversation, and a missing field does not prove misconduct. The sample is local and should not be generalized. Choose one repair, name the owner, and test it on the next set of calls.
