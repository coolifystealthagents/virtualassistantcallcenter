## Headline finding

The World Health Organization describes digital health strategy as a way to connect innovation with better health outcomes. For scheduling teams, the measurable work is narrower: capture the right intake fields, book the right slot, and make the next step visible.

## Methodology

The framework separates activity metrics from outcome metrics. Count calls and bookings, but also audit whether the appointment type, contact details, consent state, and handoff destination were correct. This avoids rewarding fast but inaccurate scheduling.

## Key stats and takeaways

- WHO's global digital-health strategy was adopted by the World Health Assembly in 2020.
- Track booking accuracy separately from average handle time.
- A reschedule reason is operational data, not a failure by itself.

## Metric set

Use five fields: answered calls, qualified scheduling requests, completed bookings, confirmed bookings, and attended appointments. Add a sixth field for preventable correction, such as wrong provider, wrong appointment type, or missing prerequisite.

The [healthcare intake checklist](/research/healthcare-appointment-intake-checklist) explains which fields should be treated as required versus optional. For non-healthcare teams, the same principle applies: define a small canonical record before optimizing speed.

## Handoff design

Every booking should produce a confirmation, a local-time appointment value, and an owner for exceptions. If a caller asks for clinical advice, a scheduler should use a preapproved handoff rather than improvising. WHO's [telemedicine guidance](https://www.who.int/news/item/10-11-2022-who-issues-consolidated-guide-to-running-effective-telemedicine-services) emphasizes governance, workforce, and compliance as part of the service design.

## FAQ

### Is average handle time the primary KPI?

No. It is a diagnostic measure. Booking accuracy and completed handoffs protect the actual customer outcome.

### How often should calls be audited?

Start with a small weekly sample across each shift and intent type, then increase the sample when an error pattern appears.
