## Headline finding

An intake scorecard should grade evidence, not conversational style. A reviewer needs to reproduce the caller’s intent, the approved route, the consent or preference signal, and the next owner from the record.

## Methodology

This note compares ten public guidance sources with a practical review set. Sample a defined period, record the denominator, and exclude incomplete records from rates rather than estimating missing values. External guidance supplies controls; local records supply performance.

## Key stats and takeaways

- Four required evidence groups are intent, minimum-necessary fields, route, and next action.
- Reviewers should label unknowns instead of converting them into successful outcomes.
- A scorecard is a control instrument, not a promise of accuracy or conversion.

## Scorecard design

Use one row per interaction. Record timestamp, channel, stated request, required fields, consent or preference, destination, action, exception, owner, and due time. Give each field pass, fail, or not applicable status. Keep an unresolved status separate from fail when the owner must investigate.

## Measurement table

| Measure | Definition | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intent evidence | Caller request is recorded in neutral terms | Can a second reviewer understand the request? |
| Field completeness | Required fields are present or marked unknown | Was extra data avoided? |
| Route evidence | Approved destination and reason are recorded | Can the handoff be reconstructed? |
| Next action | Owner and due point are explicit | Who acts next? |

## Controls and FAQ

Do not copy passwords, full payment data, or unrelated personal details into a general note. Escalate identity disputes, safety concerns, and unusual payment requests. The owner should approve exceptions and any customer-facing claim.

### What is the best denominator?

Use all eligible reviewed interactions and publish exclusions separately.

### Does the scorecard measure agent quality alone?

No. It also exposes unclear scripts, missing tools, and routing policy gaps.
