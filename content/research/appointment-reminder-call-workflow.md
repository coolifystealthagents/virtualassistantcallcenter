## Headline finding

Reminder quality is not merely a sent message. It is a documented attempt that uses the approved channel, protects identity, records confirmation or uncertainty, and provides a controlled rescheduling path.

## Methodology

Map the reminder policy against ten public sources, then sample eligible appointments by channel and outcome. Keep confirmed, declined, unreachable, rescheduled, and unresolved statuses distinct. Do not claim a reduction in no-shows without a defined baseline and comparison period.

## Key stats and takeaways

- Confirmation, refusal, reschedule, and unreachable are separate outcomes.
- The minimum record includes appointment reference, approved contact path, result, and next owner.
- Healthcare and other regulated workflows require owner-approved privacy and identity controls.

## Workflow controls

Confirm the permitted identity attributes before disclosing appointment details. Offer only approved changes. Read back date and time, record timezone where relevant, and route exceptions to the scheduling owner. Do not collect clinical details in a general reminder note.

## Measurement table

| Measure | Definition | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible reach | Attempts allowed by policy | Was the denominator defined? |
| Confirmation evidence | Customer response is recorded | Can the result be audited? |
| Correction rate | Schedule changes requiring repair | Did the system preserve accuracy? |
| Exception routing | Unclear or sensitive cases assigned | Who owns unresolved work? |

## Controls and FAQ

Use plain language, accessibility alternatives, and a clear opt-out or preference path. Avoid claims of guaranteed attendance. Escalate requests outside the assistant’s authority.

### Can a reminder disclose the appointment reason?

Only when the approved identity and disclosure policy allows it.

### What should an unreachable contact become?

A documented unreachable status with the policy-defined next action, not an assumed confirmation.
