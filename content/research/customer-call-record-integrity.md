## Headline finding

An actionable call record needs provenance, a clear correction path, and a disposition that another reviewer can understand. Completeness without traceability is weak evidence.

## Methodology

Define the source event, required fields, correction authority, audit trail, retention rule, and disposition vocabulary. Review ordinary and exception records for missing provenance, conflicting edits, excessive detail, and unresolved ownership. Do not treat a polished note as proof that the underlying event occurred.

## Key findings

- Retain the source and correction context needed for the approved business purpose.
- Separate an amended record from an original record; never hide a material correction.
- Consent, privacy, and retention requirements depend on context and must be confirmed by the responsible owner.

## Review model

Sample records by intent and outcome. Ask whether the reviewer can identify what the caller requested, what was changed, why it changed, who approved the action, and what remains open. Remove unnecessary sensitive data and escalate uncertainty instead of filling gaps from memory.
