# Call Recording Derived Artifact Inventory Study

Published October 13, 2026.

## Research question

Call Recording Derived Artifact Inventory Study asks whether transcripts, summaries, exports, screen captures, analytics, and backups follow the declared recording boundary. The unit of analysis, observation window, eligible population, exclusions, and intended operational decision must be registered before outcomes are inspected. The study reports evidence for a bounded call workflow; it does not certify a vendor, infer caller intent, or establish a universal performance benchmark.

## Freeze the population and rule versions

In stage 1 of Call Recording Derived Artifact Inventory Study, retain recording key, derived artifact, creation event, system, access, retention, deletion or hold state, owner, and verification. Link events with stable call and workflow identifiers rather than names or approximate timestamps. Preserve original timezones and source values, then add normalized fields separately. Report eligible, observed, excluded, unavailable, late, conflicting, and unresolved records as separate counts. This prevents a clean subset from silently representing the difficult calls most relevant to whether transcripts, summaries, exports, screen captures, analytics, and backups follow the declared recording boundary.

Stage 1 of Call Recording Derived Artifact Inventory Study, freeze the population and rule versions, uses a predeclared classification guide and a second reviewer for a planned subset. Reviewers work independently before adjudication and record why competing classifications were rejected. Segment results by workflow version, queue, channel, shift, language or network condition only when that comparison was specified and sufficiently populated. Privacy at stage 1 of Call Recording Derived Artifact Inventory Study requires pseudonymous study identifiers, minimum necessary fields, role-based access, a retention owner, and approved references to restricted recordings or transcripts. Do not copy customer profiles or credentials into an analysis file. Publish denominators and uncertainty, not worker rankings from small samples.
