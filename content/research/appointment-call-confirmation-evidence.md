## Headline finding

A confirmation is evidence only when it records what was requested, which time zone and channel were confirmed, who accepted the booking, and what remains unresolved.

## Methodology

Define request, confirmation, cancellation, reschedule, no-show, and disputed appointment states before reviewing records. Stratify by channel, time zone, appointment type, and exception. Exclude incomplete records from rate calculations and report the exclusion rule.

## Key findings

- Read-back should cover the date, time, time zone, location or channel, and next owner when those facts matter.
- Confirmation evidence and attendance outcomes are different measures.
- Health, legal, and other sensitive appointments require owner-approved escalation and minimum-necessary notes.

## Evidence model

Preserve the source request, the confirmation or correction event, the system or owner acceptance, and any later exception. A call service can collect and repeat details, but it should not infer attendance, clinical urgency, eligibility, or a successful outcome from a confirmation alone.
